About me

My interests center on incident response, application security, and threat hunting. SafeScale brings those interests together in one place: a space to follow developments, explore investigation ideas, and make security easier to understand.

I prefer to keep my identity private here. This site focuses on security topics and useful resources rather than personal details.

What you’ll find here

Cyber news links back to its original publishers. Breach headlines are reporting to evaluate, not conclusions from my own investigations. Hunt guides are starting points to adapt to your environment.

A thoughtful approach

Ask what the evidence supports. Understand the context. Share what is useful, and be clear about what is still unknown.
