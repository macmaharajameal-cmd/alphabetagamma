A fast little game about a useful security habit: spot the threat without disrupting the good stuff.

Arcade: escaped threats and friendly hits cost integrity. Five mistakes end the run. Missed shots break your streak. Every five clean hits boosts your multiplier, up to ×3.

Practice: slower targets, unlimited time, and no integrity loss. Reduced-motion settings keep target movement still. Leaving the tab pauses your patrol.
