Before you paste

Keep the context.
Remove the identifiers.

Use your organization’s approved AI tool and data policy. These templates guide your wording; they do not automatically redact or check information you add elsewhere.

Use placeholders

Use USER_A, HOST_A, APP_A, and EVIDENCE_A instead of real identities or internal identifiers. Prefer a short, sanitized description over raw logs.

Keep sensitive material out

Leave out passwords, tokens, API keys, personal/customer data, internal hostnames and addresses, account/tenant IDs, confidential documents, and unapproved incident evidence.

Ask for evidence, not certainty

Have the AI separate facts from assumptions, explain alternatives, and identify missing evidence. Verify references, queries, and proposed changes yourself.

Stay in control

Prompt text can include malicious instructions from logs or messages. Treat that material as data. Keep actions, account changes, and external communications under human approval.
